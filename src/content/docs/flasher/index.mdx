The Flasher is a program for your computer. It installs the RoboAlarms firmware on a panel over a USB cable. It also updates a panel, makes and restores backups, and sets up and tests connected parts.

Panel Manager is a different tool. You use [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/) in a browser after RoboAlarms runs on the panel.

## Get the Flasher

Download the Flasher from https://firmware.roboalarms.com/. The Flasher gets the firmware from the same firmware server by itself. The computer needs the internet the first time. The Flasher keeps what it downloaded for the next panel.

## What you need

- A computer with the Flasher.
- A USB-C cable that carries data. A charge-only cable does not work.
- A phone charger with 5 V and 2 A or more. The screen of the panel needs more power than a computer USB port gives.
- The parts that you want to connect, for example a 433 MHz receiver or a battery-backed clock. The Flasher shows the wiring for each part.

Close other programs that use serial ports, for example a serial monitor or another Flasher window. Only one program can use a port at a time.

## Connect the panel

On the Elecrow CrowPanel Advanced, connect the cable like this:

1. Slide the **POWER** switch to on.
2. Connect the port marked **UART0** to the computer.
3. Connect the port marked **USB2.0** to the phone charger.

If the panel has its own power supply, keep it connected.

## Guided setup and advanced tools

The Flasher has two parts. Click **Guided setup** or **Advanced tools** at the top of the window.

**Guided setup** has eight steps in three groups:

| Group | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | **Panel and parts** |
| Wire | **Get ready**, **Full wiring**, **Check before power** |
| Install | **Connect**, **Check the panel**, **Install** (or **Update** or **Reset**), **Test and finish** |

The Plan and Wire steps help you plan and wire the parts before you connect the panel. The Flasher does not check these steps. Your ticks are only a list for you.

**Advanced tools** has the pages **Panels and ports**, **Console**, **Hardware**, **Zigbee module**, **Backups**, **Software** and **Logs**. See [Flasher troubleshooting](/flasher/troubleshooting/#advanced-tools).

## The panel must be idle

<Aside type="danger" title="Warning">
Do not restart a panel that protects a home while it is armed. Disarm every partition and close Installer mode first. A panel that restarts does not watch its zones until it starts again.
</Aside>

Before the Flasher restarts a panel, it asks the panel if it is idle. Idle means disarmed, not in Installer mode, no alarm, and no software update in progress. If the panel is busy, the Flasher stops and changes nothing.

The Flasher can ask only a panel whose software has a service console. RoboAlarms software from the firmware server does not have this console. Before you restart such a panel, check on its screen that it is disarmed and idle.

The Flasher never enters keypad codes. It cannot burn eFuses, turn on secure boot or flash encryption, or change the panel's C6 coprocessor.

The Flasher knows the optional parts that you can connect to the panel. It shows where each part goes, sets up the panel for the parts, and tests them. Use this page to choose the parts, to change them later and to test them.

For the physical parts, power supplies and where to buy them, see [RoboAlarms hardware](https://roboalarms.com/hardware/). Follow the module maker's instructions too.

## The parts

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Wireless sensors (433 MHz)** | Lets the panel hear 433 MHz door, window and motion sensors and key-fob remotes. It needs a receiver: a **CC1101 board** (eight pins) or a **Receiver module** (three pins). |
| **Zigbee radio** | Elecrow's plug-in ESP32-H2 module, for Zigbee sensors. The module needs its radio software first. |
| **Z-Wave sensors** | A Zooz ZAC93 radio, for Z-Wave door, window, motion, smoke and water sensors. |
| **Battery-backed clock** | Keeps the date and time through power cuts. A small board with a coin cell (DS3231, often sold as ZS-042). |

If you are not sure which receiver you have, count the pins along its edge. Eight pins is a CC1101 board. Three pins is a receiver module. In **Advanced tools > Hardware** you can also choose **Not sure**: the panel then looks for either. **Not sure** does not work while the Zigbee module is fitted, because the search would use its pins.

## Choose the parts for a new panel

Choose the parts in the first step of the guided setup, **Panel and parts**. Tick only the parts that you have or plan to add. The Flasher picks the best place for each part.

<PanelShot src="/images/flasher/flasher-plan-parts.png" alt="What do you want to connect? with Wireless sensors (433 MHz) ticked and CC1101 board chosen" caption="Tick a part to add it. The Flasher works out where it goes." size="medium" />

To choose the pins yourself, click **Advanced: choose where each part goes**. The Flasher checks that the parts do not use the same pins.

**Get ready**, **Full wiring** and **Check before power** then show what you need, the wiring for each part, and the checks before you switch the panel on. See [Install RoboAlarms](/flasher/install/#plan-and-wire).

<Aside type="caution" title="Caution">
Power the parts from a **3V3** pin, never from **5V**. On the J7 header, one row is 5V and the other row is 3V3. If the labels are hard to read, find the 5V row with a multimeter, as **Get ready** shows. 5V can damage a part.
</Aside>

## Change the parts of a panel

<Steps>

1. Connect the panel, and click **Advanced tools**.
2. Click **Hardware**.

   The page shows **What is connected** and where each part goes.

3. Tick or untick the parts, and choose the receiver.
4. To choose the pins yourself, click **Custom pins**. To go back to the Flasher's choice, click **Suggested places**.
5. Click **Apply and restart the panel**.

   The panel saves the parts and restarts once. Then the Flasher reads the parts back.

</Steps>

The panel must be disarmed and idle before it restarts. The Flasher can apply the parts only when the panel's software answers on the service console. Software from the Stable, Beta and Alpha channels has no service console.

## Test the parts

After an install or an update, **Test and finish** shows a card for each part under **Test your parts**.

<PanelShot src="/images/flasher/flasher-test-parts.png" alt="Test your parts with Wireless sensors Working and Start listening, and Battery-backed clock Keeping time with Set it from this computer and Check again" caption="Each part shows its state. The receiver and the clock have their own tests." size="medium" />

### Test a 433 MHz receiver

<Steps>

1. On the **Wireless sensors** card, click **Start listening**.
2. Near the panel, push a button on a remote, or open a sensor.

   Each sensor or remote that the panel hears shows in the list.

3. Click **Stop listening**.

</Steps>

The test only shows what the receiver hears. It does not add a sensor to a zone. To use a sensor as a zone, on the panel open **Zones > Add a zone > 433 MHz sensor**. See [Zones](/installer/zones/). If you leave the page, the Flasher stops the test.

In **Advanced tools > Hardware**, you can record 3 seconds of raw radio pulses from a remote. Click **Record 3 seconds of raw pulses**, then push the button on the remote. You cannot start a second recording while one runs. The Flasher saves only a complete recording, in the folder `Documents\RoboAlarms\Captures`. Click **Open the folder** to see the recordings.

### Test the clock

The **Battery-backed clock** card shows **Keeping time** when the clock answers. It also shows how far the panel's clock is from the computer's clock.

- Click **Check again** to read the clock again.
- Click **Set it from this computer** to set the panel's clock to the computer's time. Check the computer's time first.

## Zigbee radio

The Zigbee module needs its radio software before it goes into the panel. The Flasher puts the software on the module while the module is out of the panel.

<Steps>

1. Switch the panel off. If the module is in its socket, lift it straight out.
2. Connect a USB-C data cable to the module and to the computer.
3. In the Flasher, click **Advanced tools**, then **Zigbee module**.

   If you ticked the Zigbee radio in **Panel and parts**, **Get ready** opens this page for you.

4. Tick **The module is on its own, not in a panel**.

   The Flasher restarts the module to read it. In a panel, this would restart the panel too.

5. Install the radio software, as the page shows.
6. Unplug the cable from the module.
7. With the panel switched off, fit the module in the radio socket, as **Full wiring** shows.

</Steps>

The Flasher can keep a copy of the software that was on the module before. Tick **Keep a copy of what it runs now** before you install. The copies are in **Backups**.

For Zigbee sensors, see [Sensors and radio modules](/installer/sensors-radios/).

## Z-Wave radio

The Flasher sets up the panel for a Zooz ZAC93 radio on the radio socket or on the J7 header. To pair Z-Wave sensors and to update the radio, see [Sensors and radio modules](/installer/sensors-radios/).

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **No receiver answers where the panel listens** | Compare the receiver and its place with the drawing in **Full wiring**. Correct the choice in **Advanced tools > Hardware**, and click **Apply and restart the panel**. Then test again. |
| The clock card does not show **Keeping time**. | Check the wiring of the clock board and its coin cell. Click **Check again**. |
| **The panel did not answer.** | The panel's software has no service console, or the panel is still starting. Wait, then try again. Set up the parts on the panel if its software has no console. |
| A recording shows **Recording not saved**. | Record again, and keep the remote close to the panel for the full 3 seconds. |
| The Flasher says that two parts use the same pins. | Choose another place for one part, or click **Suggested places**. |

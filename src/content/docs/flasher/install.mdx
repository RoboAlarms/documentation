Use this page to put RoboAlarms on a new panel. Install erases the whole chip. The maker's demo software and all saved settings on the panel are removed.

To keep the codes, users, zones and Wi-Fi of a panel that runs RoboAlarms, do not use Install. See [Update RoboAlarms](/flasher/update/).

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- You need the Flasher, a USB-C data cable and a phone charger with 5 V and 2 A or more. See [Flasher](/flasher/).
- You need the parts that you want to connect, and the items that **Get ready** lists for them.
- The computer needs the internet the first time, to download the firmware.
- Close other programs that use a serial port.
</Aside>

## Plan and wire

<Steps>

1. Start the Flasher, and click **Guided setup**.
2. In **Panel and parts**, click your panel.
3. Under **What do you want to connect?**, tick each part that you have or plan to add.

   For **Wireless sensors (433 MHz)**, also click the receiver that you have. If you do not add a part, tick nothing. You can add parts later.

   <PanelShot src="/images/flasher/flasher-plan-parts.png" alt="What do you want to connect? with Wireless sensors (433 MHz) ticked, CC1101 board chosen, and the Zigbee radio and Z-Wave sensors choices" caption="Tick the parts, then choose the receiver that you have." size="medium" />

4. Click **Next: get ready to wire**.
5. In **Get ready**, gather the items in **What you need**, and tick each one.

   The page also shows where each part goes on the panel. If you ticked the Zigbee radio, the Flasher puts its radio software on first. See [Connected parts](/flasher/connected-parts/#zigbee-radio).

6. Click **Next: see the full wiring**.
7. In **Full wiring**, wire each part as the drawing shows, and tick each wire.

   Click **Print or save** to keep the wiring on paper.

8. Click **Next: check before power**.
9. In **Check before power**, do each check, and tick it.
10. Click **Next: plug the panel in**.

</Steps>

The Flasher does not test the wiring in these steps. Your ticks are a list for you only.

## Connect and check the panel

<Steps>

1. Slide the **POWER** switch of the panel to on.
2. Connect **UART0** to the computer, and **USB2.0** to the phone charger.

   In **Connect**, the Flasher shows **Found a panel** and the port, for example COM5.

3. Click **Next: check the panel**.
4. If the panel is not your alarm panel, or you are not sure, unplug it now.
5. In **Check the panel**, click **Check this panel**.

   The panel restarts for a moment, so that the Flasher can read its chip. Nothing is written. A panel that runs RoboAlarms with a service console answers by itself and does not restart.

6. If the Flasher asks **Which board is this?**, click your panel.

   The Flasher shows **Ready for RoboAlarms** and the version.

7. Click **Next: review**.

</Steps>

## Install

<Aside type="caution" title="Caution">
Keep **Back up the panel first** ticked on a new panel. Install erases the chip. The backup is the only way back to the maker's demo software.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. In **Install**, check the **Panel**, **Software** and **Parts** rows.

   To change a row, click **Change** beside it.

   <PanelShot src="/images/flasher/flasher-install-review.png" alt="Ready to install RoboAlarms 0.8.22 with the Panel, Software and Parts rows, the five steps under What happens, and Back up the panel first ticked" caption="What happens lists the steps of the install. Back up the panel first is ticked." size="medium" />

2. Check that **Back up the panel first** is ticked.

   The backup goes to the folder `Documents\RoboAlarms\Backups`.

3. Click **Install RoboAlarms** and the version.

   The Flasher backs up the panel and checks the backup. Then it erases the chip, writes the firmware and the voice of the language, and starts the panel. Last, it sets up the panel for your parts, with one more restart.

   <PanelShot src="/images/flasher/flasher-install-progress.png" alt="Installing RoboAlarms, 60 %, about 2 minutes left" caption="The bar shows how far the install is." size="medium" />

4. Keep the cable and the power connected until the Flasher shows **RoboAlarms is running on your panel**.

</Steps>

If the cable comes out or the computer sleeps, the panel is not damaged. Connect it again and start the install again.

## Test and finish

<Steps>

1. On the panel's touchscreen, join your Wi-Fi.
2. On the touchscreen, change the installer code and the master code when the panel asks.

   The factory codes are public. Change them before the panel protects a home.

3. In the Flasher, test each part under **Test your parts**.

   See [Connected parts](/flasher/connected-parts/#test-the-parts).

4. Click **Finish**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
The Flasher shows **RoboAlarms is running on your panel**. The touchscreen shows the first setup. Continue with [Run the first setup](/installer/first-setup/).
</Aside>

Software from the firmware server's Stable, Beta and Alpha channels has no service console. Then the Flasher cannot set up or test the parts after the install, and it shows **The panel did not answer**. Set up the parts later in **Advanced tools > Hardware**, or on the panel.

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect** finds no panel. | Check the **POWER** switch, the **UART0** port and the cable. Click **Nothing showing up?** for more help. See [Flasher troubleshooting](/flasher/troubleshooting/). |
| The install stops during the erase or the write. | Keep the panel powered. Connect it again, and run Install again. The panel has no working firmware until an install finishes. |
| **This panel already runs RoboAlarms firmware, and an install erases its codes, users, zones and Wi-Fi.** | Use [Update](/flasher/update/) to keep them. Choose Install only if you want to erase the panel. |
| The screen stays black after the install. | Connect **USB2.0** to a phone charger with 5 V and 2 A or more. Then switch the panel off and on with **POWER**. |

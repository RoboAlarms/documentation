An update writes new firmware and keeps the codes, users, zones, Wi-Fi and history of the panel. Use this page to update a panel over USB with the Flasher.

The Flasher can update a panel only when the panel's software answers it on the service console. Software from the Stable, Beta and Alpha channels has no service console. Update such a panel on its own screen or in [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/updates/). See [Updates and storage](/user/updates-storage/).

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- The panel runs RoboAlarms with a service console, for example a Dev build on a test panel.
- You need the Flasher, a USB-C data cable and a phone charger. See [Flasher](/flasher/).
- Disarm every partition, close Installer mode and wait until no update is in progress on the panel.
</Aside>

## Update the panel

<Steps>

1. Start the Flasher, and click **Guided setup**.
2. Go through **Panel and parts**, **Get ready**, **Full wiring** and **Check before power**.

   Keep the parts that the panel has. If you add a part, wire it as **Full wiring** shows.

3. Connect the panel to the computer, and click **Next: check the panel**.

   **Check the panel** shows **Your panel runs RoboAlarms** and the version. The panel does not restart for this check.

4. Check that the panel shows **Disarmed and idle: safe to update**.
5. Click **Next: review**.
6. In **Update**, click **Update to RoboAlarms** and the version.

   <PanelShot src="/images/flasher/flasher-update-review.png" alt="Ready to update to RoboAlarms 0.8.22 with Update to RoboAlarms 0.8.22 chosen, Reset to factory defaults, and What happens" caption="Update to RoboAlarms keeps codes, users, zones, Wi-Fi and history." size="medium" />

7. Check the **Panel**, **Software** and **Parts** rows.
8. Click **Update to** and the version.

   The Flasher checks that the panel is still disarmed. Then it writes and checks the new software, updates the voice, and restarts the panel. If you chose parts, it sets them up with one more restart.

9. Keep the cable and the power connected until **Test and finish** shows that the panel is updated.
10. Test the parts. See [Connected parts](/flasher/connected-parts/#test-the-parts).
11. Click **Finish**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
**Test and finish** shows **Updated** and the new version. On the panel, **Settings > About** shows the same version, and the zones and users are the same as before.
</Aside>

## Go back to an older version

If the chosen version is older than the version on the panel, the Flasher asks **Go back to** and the version. Settings made with the newer version can be lost or not understood by the older one. Click **Go back** only if you want the older version.

## If the flash layout is different

Before it writes, the Flasher compares how the new firmware lays out the flash with the layout on the panel. If they are different, the Flasher stops and writes nothing. It shows **This firmware lays the flash out differently from the one on the panel, so it cannot be updated in place.**

Then only Install or Reset can put this version on the panel. Both erase the codes, users, zones and Wi-Fi. Make a backup first. See [Back up, reset and restore](/flasher/backups/).

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The Flasher shows **No RoboAlarms software yet** for a panel that runs RoboAlarms. | The panel's software has no service console. Update the panel on its screen or in [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/updates/). Do not choose Install: it erases the panel. |
| **The panel's firmware does not answer, so it cannot be updated in place.** | Check the panel in **Advanced tools > Console**. If the panel has no service console, update it on its screen or in Panel Manager. |
| **The panel is armed away: disarm it first.** or another busy message | Disarm the panel, close Installer mode, and wait for alarms or updates to end. Then click **Look again**. |
| **The panel is checking a new firmware (probation): wait a minute and look again.** | Wait one minute, then click **Look again**. |
| The update stops or the cable comes out. | Connect the same panel again, and run Update again. Do not run Install: it erases the settings. |

Pairing lets Home Assistant see the panel. Sharing is a separate permission in the other direction: it lets the panel use Home Assistant entities that you choose. Use this page to choose the sensors that the panel can use as zones and the devices that it can control.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- You need a Home Assistant administrator account.
- The panel is paired with Home Assistant. See [Install and pair Home Assistant](/integrations/home-assistant-setup/).
</Aside>

## Choose what the panel can use

<Steps>

1. In Home Assistant, open **Settings > Devices & services**.
2. Open the **RoboAlarms** integration.
3. Click the gear button of the panel entry.

   Home Assistant shows **What the panel may see and do**.

4. Under **Entities the panel may use as zones**, click **Add entity** and choose a binary sensor.
5. Do step 4 again for each sensor that the panel uses as a zone.
6. Under **Entities the panel may control**, add each device that the panel can operate.
7. Click **Submit**.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/home-assistant/share-entities.png" alt="Home Assistant dialog What the panel may see and do with one sensor for zones and one light for control" caption="The zone list and the control list are separate; an entity in one list is not in the other." size="small" />

Both lists are empty at first. If both lists are empty, the panel gets nothing from Home Assistant. The panel gets only the name, area, device class and state of each entity that you choose. Do not share the panel's own RoboAlarms entities back to the panel.

## Entities the panel may use as zones

This list takes binary sensors, for example door, window and motion sensors. To connect a shared sensor to a zone on the panel, see [Use Home Assistant sensors](/installer/home-assistant-zones/).

A Home Assistant sensor needs Home Assistant and the network. Do not use it for a fire, CO, panic or other 24-hour zone.

## Entities the panel may control

This list takes these kinds of entities: lights, switches, input booleans, fans, climate entities, covers, locks, valves, scenes and scripts. The panel shows them as tiles on its **Smart Home** screen. For how people use the tiles, see [Control household devices](/user/smart-home/).

- To unlock a lock, or to open a cover or a valve, the panel always asks for a user code.
- On the panel, **Settings > Smart Home** can make more devices ask for a master code before a change.
- The panel does only the actions of each entity kind. It gets no other access to Home Assistant.

<Aside type="caution" title="Caution">
Check what a scene or script does before you share it. The panel runs the scene or script, but it cannot see or limit what the scene or script does to other devices. A script can unlock a door without the panel's code prompt.
</Aside>

## Rooms on the panel

The rooms on the panel's **Smart Home** screen belong to the panel. A shared device first shows in the room of its Home Assistant area. The device follows its area in Home Assistant until someone puts it in a room on the panel. After that, the panel keeps the device in its panel room. A device with no area and no room shows under **Other**.

To remove a permission, remove the entity from the list and click **Submit**.

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
A sensor that you shared shows in **Pick the sensor** when you set a zone source to **Home Assistant sensor**. A device that you shared for control shows as a tile on **Smart Home**.
</Aside>

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A sensor is not in the zone list in Home Assistant. | Only binary sensors can be zones. Choose a binary sensor. |
| A shared device does not show on **Smart Home**. | Check that the entity is in **Entities the panel may control**. Check that the panel is still paired. |
| A device tile shows **Offline**. | Check the entity in Home Assistant. It must not show as unavailable. |
| A device is in the wrong room on the panel. | Move it on the panel with **Arrange**, or change its area in Home Assistant if nobody moved it on the panel. |

Use this page to set up a new panel from the start. Each step links to the full instructions and tells you how to know that the step worked.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- A computer with an internet connection and a USB data cable.
- The name and password of your home Wi-Fi network.
- At least one door, window or motion sensor to connect.
- Time to do the steps in order. Each step needs the step before it.
</Aside>

<Aside type="danger" title="Warning">
Read [Safety and limits](/start/safety/) before you rely on the panel. RoboAlarms is not a monitoring service and does not call emergency services.
</Aside>

## Set up the panel

<Steps>

1. **Get the hardware.** Find the panel, the power supply and the parts that you want on [RoboAlarms hardware](https://roboalarms.com/hardware/). Do the physical installation and the wiring as the maker of each part tells you.

   **How to tell it worked:** The panel turns on when it gets power.

2. **Install the firmware with the Flasher.** Connect the panel to your computer with USB. Then follow [Install RoboAlarms](/flasher/install/). The Flasher gets the firmware from the RoboAlarms firmware server.

   **How to tell it worked:** The Flasher finishes its last step, and the panel restarts and shows **Welcome**.

3. **Do the first setup on the touchscreen.** Push **Start** and follow the steps on the screen. Choose the role of the panel, join Wi-Fi, choose the time zone, change both factory codes, and name the system. For each step, see [First setup](/installer/first-setup/).

   <PanelShot src="/images/panel/quick-start-welcome.png" alt="The Welcome screen of the first setup, with a list of the setup steps and a Start button" caption="The Welcome screen lists each step of the first setup." />

   **How to tell it worked:** The panel shows the **Security** screen with the correct time. The top bar does not show **Factory codes · Tap to change**.

4. **Add your first zone.** Open **Settings > About > Installer**, enter the installer code, and open **Zones**. Then follow [Zones](/installer/zones/) to add a zone and connect its sensor.

   **How to tell it worked:** On the **Zones** screen, the zone changes from **Closed** to **Open** when you open the door or window.

5. **Do a walk test.** Open **Settings > Walk test**, enter the master code, and push **Start walk test**. Open and close each door and window, and walk past each motion sensor. Push **End test** when you finish.

   **How to tell it worked:** The panel beeps for each zone, and the count shows that every zone was tested.

6. **Arm and disarm one time.** Push **Stay**, enter your code and push the check mark. When the panel shows **Armed Stay**, enter your code and push the check mark again. See [Arm and disarm](/user/arming-disarming/).

   **How to tell it worked:** The panel shows **Armed Stay**, then **Disarmed** after you enter your code.

7. **Optional: connect more.** Turn on [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/) to use the panel from a browser. Connect [Home Assistant](/integrations/home-assistant-setup/), [MQTT](/installer/mqtt/) or [phone notifications](/installer/notifications/) if you use them.

   **How to tell it worked:** The browser opens the Panel Manager sign-in page, or Home Assistant shows the panel as connected.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
The panel shows **Disarmed** and **Ready to arm** on the **Security** screen. Each zone that you added changes state when you open and close it. Then do the full [commissioning checklist](/installer/commissioning/) before you hand over the panel.
</Aside>

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The Flasher does not find the panel. | Use a USB data cable and close other programs that use the port. See [Flasher troubleshooting](/flasher/troubleshooting/). |
| The panel screen stays black after the install. | Check the power supply. See [Flasher troubleshooting](/flasher/troubleshooting/). |
| The panel does not join Wi-Fi. | Push **Skip for now** and continue. Join Wi-Fi later in **Settings > Wi-Fi and network**. |
| A zone does not change when you open the door. | Check the sensor and its connection. See [Zones](/installer/zones/). |
| The panel shows **Not ready**. | Open **Zones** and close the zone that is open. |

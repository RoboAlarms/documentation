This page answers common questions about RoboAlarms. Each answer links to the page with the full information.

## About the system

### Does RoboAlarms need the internet?

No. The panel makes every alarm decision itself and keeps working without the internet. The time from the internet, update checks and phone notifications need the internet. See [Wi-Fi, network and time](/user/network-time/#without-wi-fi).

### Does the panel call the police or a monitoring center?

No. RoboAlarms is not a monitoring service, and the panel does not call emergency services. Phone notifications can tell you about an alarm, but they can arrive late or not at all. See [Safety and limits](/start/safety/).

### Is RoboAlarms a certified alarm system?

No. RoboAlarms is do-it-yourself software. It is not a certified or listed alarm system. Some delay settings follow alarm standards, but these settings do not certify an installation. See [Safety and limits](/start/safety/).

### Do I need Home Assistant?

No. The panel works without Home Assistant. Panel automations run on the panel itself. You need Home Assistant only to use its sensors as zones or to control its devices on **Smart Home**. See [Create panel automations](/user/automations/).

### What happens if the Wi-Fi stops?

The alarm keeps working, with its wired and radio sensors. A zone that uses a Home Assistant sensor shows **Not reporting**, and the panel cannot detect an opening on it. A keypad panel shows **No system communication** and cannot arm until the Wi-Fi is back. See [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting/#the-keypad-panel-shows-no-system-communication).

### What happens if the power fails?

The panel runs on its backup battery and shows **Mains power lost**. Restore the power before the battery is empty. After a restart, the panel goes back to its earlier armed state, unless the installer changed this. See [Delays and arming](/installer/system-options/#power-and-walk-test).

### Is my data sent anywhere?

The panel has no cloud account. It sends data only to the services that you turn on: your phone notification service, your MQTT broker and your Home Assistant. The panel also asks the update server for new versions and gets the time from a time server. See [Connect other systems](/integrations/).

## Sensors, sirens and keypads

### Can I use my old wired sensors?

Yes. Wire each contact between a terminal and GND, and choose its **Contact** type on the panel. On a terminal that can measure the loop, you can use end-of-line resistors. Then the panel detects a cut or shorted wire. See [Wired zones and end-of-line resistors](/installer/zones/#wired-zones-and-end-of-line-resistors).

### Which wireless sensors can I use?

The panel can use 433 MHz, Zigbee and Z-Wave sensors, with the matching radio module. It can also use binary sensors from Home Assistant. Do not use a 433 MHz or Home Assistant sensor for fire, CO or panic protection. See [Sensors and radio modules](/installer/sensors-radios/).

### Which sirens can I connect?

You can use wired sirens and strobes through a relay or MOSFET module, and 433 MHz, Zigbee and Z-Wave sirens. The panel's own speaker always sounds too. See [Sirens and outputs](/installer/sirens/).

### Can I have more than one keypad?

Yes. Another RoboAlarms panel on the same Wi-Fi can be a keypad for your alarm panel, for example at a second door. A keypad panel shows the alarm state and arms and disarms its partition. See [Keypad panels](/installer/keypads/).

### Can parts of the house arm separately?

Yes. The installer can set up to four partitions, for example a house and a studio. Each partition arms and disarms on its own. See [Partitions](/user/partitions/).

## Codes and access

### Who can change what?

Each code has a level. A user code arms and disarms. A master code also manages users and household settings. The installer code changes how the alarm works, but it never arms or disarms. See [Who can do what](/reference/permissions/).

### I forgot my code. What do I do?

Ask the person who manages users to give you a new code. If you lost the master code, ask the installer. Do not use a factory reset to recover a code. See [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting/#you-forgot-your-code).

### Can I see the panel from my phone?

Yes, on your home network. Panel Manager runs on the panel, and you open it in a browser. It has no cloud relay. From outside your home, you need a VPN into your home network. Phone notifications and Home Assistant can also tell you about the panel. See [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/).

### What does a duress code do?

A duress code disarms like a normal code. It also records a private event that a connected service can report. It does not send help by itself. See [Users and codes](/user/users-codes/#what-a-code-can-do).

## Updates and settings

### How do I update the panel?

Open **Settings > About > Software update** and push **Install**. You need a system master code or the installer code. Keypad panels update with the alarm panel. You can also update over USB with the Flasher. See [Software updates and SD card](/user/updates-storage/).

### Can I change the language of the panel?

Yes. Open **Settings > Time and language** and push **Language**. The voice announcements speak English (United States) in every screen language. See [Wi-Fi, network and time](/user/network-time/#set-the-time-zone-language-and-time-server).

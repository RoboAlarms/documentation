## The parts of RoboAlarms

| Part | What it is | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **RoboAlarms Panel** | The touchscreen on your wall. It holds the alarm and works without the internet. | [Use your panel](/user/getting-started/) |
| **Flasher** | An app for your computer. It installs and updates the firmware over USB. | [Flasher](/flasher/) |
| **Panel Manager** | Pages that the panel shows in a browser on your home network. | [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/) |
| **Home Assistant integration** | An optional connection to Home Assistant. | [Home Assistant](/integrations/) |

For the hardware list and project news, go to [roboalarms.com](https://roboalarms.com).

Use this page for the everyday Panel Manager pages: arm and disarm, zones, activity, troubles and the panel settings. The menu shows only the pages that your role can use. See [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/#the-pages).

Panel Manager does the same things as the touchscreen. For what arming levels, bypass and troubles mean, see the user guide: [Arm and disarm](/user/arming-disarming/), [Zones and bypass](/user/zones-bypass/) and [Alarms and troubles](/user/alarms-troubles/).

## Arm the panel

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- Sign in as a standard user, a partition master or a system master. The installer does not arm or disarm.
- **Overview** shows **Ready to arm**.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Click **Overview**.
2. Click **Away**, **Stay** or **Night**.

   Panel Manager shows the zones that the level watches, the exit time and the options.

3. If you want, turn on an option:
   - **No entry delay**: doors sound the alarm at once.
   - **Silent exit** (Away only): no beeps, and twice the exit time.
   - **Bypass** and the zone names: the open zones are off until the next disarm.
4. Click **Arm Away**, **Arm Stay** or **Arm Night**.

   **Overview** shows the exit time. Then it shows the armed state.

</Steps>

Panel Manager does not ask for a code. It uses your role and your partitions, as your code does at the panel. If a zone is open and cannot be bypassed, the arm button stays off.

## Disarm the panel

<Steps>

1. Click **Overview**.
2. Click **Disarm**.

   Panel Manager asks **Disarm** and the partition name.

3. Click **Disarm**.

</Steps>

During an alarm, **Overview** shows **Disarm and silence**. After the alarm, click **Clear alarm**.

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
**Overview** shows **Ready to arm** or **Not ready**, and **Recent activity** shows **Disarmed** and your name.
</Aside>

## Zones

**Zones** shows each zone and its state. Click **All**, **Open**, **Bypassed** or **Trouble** to filter the list. Click **What these words mean** for the zone states.

To bypass a zone, click **Bypass** on the zone. To end the bypass, click **Unbypass**. You can bypass only while the partition is disarmed. A bypass ends at the next disarm.

The installer programs zones in **Installer > Zones**. See [Installer programming](/panel-manager/installer/#program-a-zone).

## Activity and troubles

**Activity** shows the most recent events on the panel, newest first. Click **All**, **Alarms**, **Troubles**, **Arming**, **Bypass** or **System** to filter the list.

**Troubles** shows the active troubles and **What the panel watches**: mains power, backup battery, network, reports, clock, wireless sensors, siren, storage and the panel cover.

When the panel beeps for a trouble, click **Acknowledge** to stop the beeps. A trouble stays in the list until it is fixed.

## Display and sound

<Steps>

1. Click **Display and sound**.
2. Change the settings, for example **Screen brightness**, **Screen saver**, **Night hours** or **Beeps volume**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

   Panel Manager shows the old and new values.

4. Check the values, and click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

The settings are the same as **Settings > Screen and sound** on the panel. Alarm sounds do not change. **Language** changes the words on the panel's screen. Only a system master, a partition master or the installer can change the language and the region. See [Screen and sound](/user/display-sound/).

The **Announcements** card shows the voice pack of the panel. A system master or the installer can install a signed `.robovoice` file there. The language of the Panel Manager pages is set for this browser only.

## Date and time

Everyone can see the time zone and the time server. A system master, a partition master or the installer can change them.

<Steps>

1. Click **Date and time**.
2. Choose the **Time zone**.

   If your time zone is not in the list, choose **Other: type a POSIX rule**, and type the **POSIX time-zone rule**.

3. Type the **Time server**, for example `pool.ntp.org` or your router.
4. Click **Save changes**, check the values, and click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

If the panel can keep the time by itself, the page shows **Set the date and time**. Click **Use this computer's time**, or type the **New date and time**. Then click **Set**. The time server corrects the time at its next sync. See [Network and time](/user/network-time/).

## Storage

**Storage** shows the SD card and the panel's internal flash. A system master or the installer can use the card actions:

- **Check** looks for file-system errors.
- **Eject** prepares the card for removal. Wait for **Safe to remove** before you take the card out.
- **Erase and format…** deletes every file on the card. Type `ERASE SD CARD` to confirm. The panel's settings, users and event log are in its own flash and stay.

## System health and walk test

**System health** shows live readings: memory, uptime, firmware version, display, network and power. Click **Refresh** for new values.

A system master, a partition master or the installer can start a walk test in **Walk test**. Click **Start walk test**, then trip each sensor once. The page lists each zone as tested. Under **On this phone**, you can turn on **Beep**, **Vibrate** and **Say zone names**. Click **End walk test** to finish. See [Commission and hand over](/installer/commissioning/).

## Integrations

A system master or the installer opens **Integrations**. The page shows each integration in this firmware and its state, for example **Home Assistant**, **MQTT**, **ntfy** and **Pushover**. Click **Set up** to open one.

Saved passwords and keys are never shown again. An empty field keeps the saved value.

- For Home Assistant, see [Pair with Home Assistant](/installer/home-assistant/).
- For MQTT, see [MQTT](/installer/mqtt/).
- For phone alerts, see [Notifications](/installer/notifications/).

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **The panel is armed or an alarm is sounding** | Settings change only while every partition is disarmed and quiet. Disarm, then try again. |
| The arming buttons are not on **Overview**. | You are signed in as the installer, or your role cannot arm this partition. |
| **Bypass** is off. | Disarm the partition first. A zone that cannot be bypassed has no button. |
| A page is not in the menu. | Your role cannot use it. See [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/#the-pages). |

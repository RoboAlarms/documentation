Panel Manager is a website that the panel serves itself. Open it in a browser on the same network as the panel, or over a VPN that you trust. It needs no cloud account, no Home Assistant and no other server.

Use Panel Manager to see the alarm state, arm and disarm, and change settings from a computer or phone. Each person sees only the pages and partitions that their role allows.

## Turn on Panel Manager

Panel Manager is off on a new panel. The installer turns it on at the panel.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- You need the installer code.
- Disarm every partition.
- The computer must be on the same network as the panel, or on a VPN to it.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. On the panel, open **Settings > About > Installer**, and enter the installer code.
2. Under **This panel**, turn on **Panel Manager**.

   The row shows the address of the panel and the fingerprint of its certificate.

3. Write down the address and the fingerprint.
4. To sign in as the installer, turn on **Browser sign-in** below the switch, and set a password.

   See [Installer access](/installer/installer-access/).

</Steps>

## Open Panel Manager and sign in

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- You need your user name and your browser password. Your user name is your name in **Settings > Users** on the panel.
- The browser password is not your keypad code. See [Browser accounts](/panel-manager/accounts/) if you have no password yet.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. In the browser, open the address of the panel with `https://`, for example https://192.0.2.42.

   The panel uses its own certificate, so the browser shows a warning the first time.

2. Compare the certificate fingerprint in the browser with the fingerprint on the panel.
3. If the two are the same, accept the certificate in the browser.

   Panel Manager shows **Sign in**.

   <PanelShot src="/images/panel-manager/pm-sign-in.png" alt="Panel Manager Sign in card with User name Master, an empty Password field and the Sign in button" caption="Sign in with your name on the panel and your browser password." size="small" />

4. Type your **User name** and your **Password**.
5. Click **Sign in**.

   Panel Manager shows **Overview**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Caution">
Do not accept the certificate if the fingerprints are different. Another device on the network can be in between.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
**Overview** shows the alarm state of your partitions. The top bar shows **Connected**, and the menu shows your name and role at the bottom.
</Aside>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel-manager/pm-overview.png" alt="Panel Manager Overview with Ready to arm, Away, Stay and Night, Chime, Zones All closed, Troubles None, Power Plugged in, Connection Online and recent activity" caption="Overview shows the alarm state, the arming buttons and recent activity." />

If you open the address with `http://`, the panel sends the browser to `https://`.

## The pages

The menu shows only the pages that your role can use.

| Menu group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| (top) | **Overview**, **Remote keypad**, **Activity**, **Zones**, **Troubles** |
| **Household** | **People and codes**, **Integrations**, **Automations** |
| **Panel settings** | **Display and sound**, **Network**, **Date and time**, **Storage** |
| **Installer** | **Zones**, **Z-Wave**, **Zigbee**, **Partitions and devices**, **System options**, **Access and maintenance** |
| **System** | **System health**, **Walk test**, **Software updates**, **Developer** |

| Role | What the person can use |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | Overview, Remote keypad, Activity, Zones, Troubles, Display and sound, Date and time (to read), Storage (to read), System health. Arms, disarms and bypasses in their own partitions. |
| Partition master | The standard pages, and **People and codes** for their own partitions, **Network**, **Date and time** and **Walk test**. |
| System master | The partition master pages for all partitions, and **Integrations**, **Automations**, **Software updates** and the SD card actions in **Storage**. |
| Installer | All pages, and the **Installer** group. **Developer** shows only while developer tools are on. The installer does not arm or disarm. |

Arm-only, guest and duress users cannot sign in to Panel Manager.

The panel checks the role on every request. A copied page address does not give more access.

## While the panel is armed

While a partition is armed, from its exit time on, or an alarm sounds, Panel Manager does not change settings. You can still arm, disarm, use the remote keypad, change your own password and sign out. Disarm every partition to change settings.

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The browser cannot open the address. | Check the address on the panel in **Settings > About > Installer**. Check that the computer is on the same network. |
| The page says that keypad codes do not sign in. | Type your browser password, not your keypad code. |
| No password yet. | Ask the installer or a master to turn on your **Browser sign-in**. See [Browser accounts](/panel-manager/accounts/). |
| After five wrong passwords, sign-in waits. | Wait one minute, then type the password again. |
| The page shows **Browser access is off**. | The installer turns **Panel Manager** on again at the panel. |

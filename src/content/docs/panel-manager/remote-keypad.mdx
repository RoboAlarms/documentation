**Remote keypad** shows the panel's own screen in the browser. You use it as if you stand at the panel. Use this page to control the screen from the browser, and to know what the person at the panel can do.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- Sign in to [Panel Manager](/panel-manager/).
- Only one browser can use the remote keypad at a time.
</Aside>

## Use the remote keypad

<Steps>

1. Click **Remote keypad**.

   The panel's screen shows in the browser. You have control at once.

   <PanelShot src="/images/panel-manager/pm-remote-keypad.png" alt="Remote keypad page with the panel's Security screen, the red banner Remote: Master · Tap to pause, and Video (H.264) and Still pictures (JPEG)" caption="The red banner on the panel shows who uses the remote keypad." />

2. Click a button on the screen to push it.

   To hold a button, for example on **Panic**, keep the mouse button down. To scroll, drag.

3. When the panel shows a text box or a code pad, type on the computer keyboard.

   The keyboard sends letters, digits, Backspace, Enter and Escape. You can also paste text.

4. To finish, open another page or close the browser tab.

   The panel signs out of anything that you opened.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
The bottom of the page shows **You have control**. The panel shows a red banner with your name.
</Aside>

Settings on the panel still ask for a code, as at the panel. Anyone who can see your screen can see what you type. Do not share your screen while you enter a code.

## Video or still pictures

Click **Video (H.264)** for a moving picture. If the browser cannot show the video, or the video looks wrong, click **Still pictures (JPEG)**. The browser remembers your choice.

If the browser tab is in the background, the pictures stop. After about a minute, the panel ends the session. Open **Remote keypad** again to connect again.

## What the person at the panel sees

While you use the remote keypad, the panel shows a red banner: **Remote:** and your name, then **Tap to pause**.

- When someone touches the panel, the panel takes over for a moment. Your clicks wait.
- While someone types a code or uses a protected page at the panel, your clicks wait until they finish.
- The person at the panel can push the banner to pause you. The panel then shows **Someone is using this keypad remotely** and two choices:
  - **Allow** lets you continue.
  - **Turn off remote access** turns off Panel Manager. Only the installer can turn it on again.

While the remote keypad is paused, the banner shows **Remote keypad paused · Tap for choices**. The pause stays when you connect again. The person at the panel must push **Allow** first.

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Remote keypad** is not in the menu. | This panel cannot share its screen. Use the other Panel Manager pages. |
| The page says that another person is using the remote keypad now. | Wait until they finish, then try again. |
| Your clicks do nothing. | Someone uses the panel, or the person at the panel paused you. Ask them to push **Allow**. |
| The video does not move or looks wrong. | Click **Still pictures (JPEG)**. |
| Panel Manager stops answering after a pause. | The person at the panel turned off remote access. The installer turns **Panel Manager** on again at the panel. |

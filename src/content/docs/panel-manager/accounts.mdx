Each person signs in to Panel Manager with a user name and a browser password. Use this page to give a person a sign-in, to set or change a password, and to know who can manage whom.

The user name is the person's name in **Settings > Users** on the panel. The browser password is not the keypad code. A keypad code, also the installer code, never signs in to Panel Manager.

## Who can sign in

| Role | Browser sign-in | Manages users |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Installer | Yes. The user name is **Installer** from the factory. | Every user. |
| System master | Yes | Every user except the installer. |
| Partition master | Yes | Itself, and users whose partitions are all inside its own partitions. |
| Standard | Yes | No one. |
| Arm only, Guest, Duress | No | No one. |

A person who manages another person can turn on their sign-in. This works at the panel in **Settings > Users** and in Panel Manager in **People and codes**. The installer turns on their own sign-in at the panel, in **Settings > About > Installer**. See [Installer access](/installer/installer-access/).

## Give a person a sign-in

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- Sign in as the installer, a system master or a partition master.
- Disarm every partition.
- The person has a role that can sign in.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Click **People and codes**.
2. Click the person.

   To add a new person, click **Add a person**. Then type the **Name**, choose **What this code can do**, and type a **Code** of 4 to 10 digits.

3. Under **Panel Manager**, turn on **Browser sign-in**.

   The line under it shows the user name.

4. In **Password**, type a password of 12 to 64 characters.

   A few unrelated words make a good password. Do not use a keypad code.

5. Choose if the person must change the password:
   - Turn on **Ask them to change it when they sign in** for a temporary password.
   - Turn it off if the person keeps this password.
6. Click **Save**.

   For a new person, click **Add this person**.

7. Give the person the user name and the password in a private way.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
The list in **People and codes** shows **Browser sign-in** beside the person. With a temporary password, the person's page shows **Waiting for their own password** until they choose their own.
</Aside>

A person with a temporary password must choose their own password at the first sign-in. Until then, Panel Manager shows nothing else.

## Set a new password for a person

If a person forgot their password, a person who manages them sets a new one.

<PanelShot src="/images/panel-manager/pm-people-browser-sign-in.png" alt="Panel Manager card with Browser sign-in on, User name Sam, Password with Set a new password, and Remove this person" caption="Set a new password is for a person who forgot theirs." size="medium" />

<Steps>

1. In **People and codes**, click the person.
2. Under **Panel Manager**, click **Set a new password**.
3. Type the **New password**.
4. Turn on **Ask them to change it when they sign in**.
5. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

To stop, click **Keep their current password** before you save.

## Change your own password

<Steps>

1. At the bottom of the menu, click **Change password**.
2. Type your **Current password**.
3. Type your **New password**.
4. Click **Change password**.

   Panel Manager shows **Password changed.** Your other browsers are signed out.

</Steps>

Your keypad code does not change. If you do not know your current password, ask a person who manages you to set a new one.

## Turn off a sign-in or remove a person

- To stop a person's browser sign-in, open the person, turn off **Browser sign-in**, and click **Save**.
- To remove a person, open the person and click **Remove** under **Remove this person**. Their code and their browser sign-in stop working at once. You cannot remove yourself, the installer (user 01) or the system master (user 02).

## Sign-in rules

- After five wrong passwords, every sign-in waits one minute. Each more wrong password doubles the wait, up to 15 minutes. One hour without a wrong password clears the count.
- A wrong password that you type to confirm a change counts too.
- A session ends after 20 minutes without use, and 8 hours after the sign-in at the latest.
- The panel keeps up to four sessions. A new sign-in ends the oldest session when all four are in use.
- A change to a person's name, role, code or password signs that person out. Turning off **Browser sign-in** signs them out too.
- Some changes, for example remote access, keypads and backups, ask for your own browser password again. Panel Manager asks if you did not type it in the last 10 minutes.

Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu when you finish on a shared computer.

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Too many wrong passwords. Wait a minute and try again.** | Wait, then type the password once. Do not try many times. |
| **A browser password has at least 12 characters.** | Type a longer password. |
| **You don't manage this person.** | Ask the installer or a system master. A partition master manages only users inside its own partitions. |
| **Arm-only, guest and duress codes can't sign in to the Panel Manager.** | Choose another role for the person if they need a sign-in. |
| The person signs in but sees only a password page. | The password is temporary. The person chooses their own password first. |

An automation is a rule that the panel runs by itself: when something happens, the panel does one or two things. Use this page to manage automations in Panel Manager. For what triggers and actions do, see [Automations](/user/automations/) in the user guide.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- Sign in as the system master or the installer. Other roles do not see **Automations**.
- Disarm every partition. While a partition is armed or an alarm sounds, you can only look at the automations. They keep running.
- The devices that you want to use are connected, for example through Home Assistant, Z-Wave or Zigbee.
</Aside>

## Add an automation

<Steps>

1. Click **Automations**.

   The list shows each automation as a sentence, and how many of the 16 places are in use.

2. Click **Add an automation**.

   Panel Manager shows ideas to start from.

   <PanelShot src="/images/panel-manager/pm-automations-ideas.png" alt="Add an automation with ideas: Turn on a light when a door opens, Lock up when you arm Away, Turn on lights when an alarm sounds, Close the water when a leak is found, Arm Night at bedtime, Start from scratch" caption="Pick an idea, or Start from scratch." size="medium" />

3. Click an idea, or click **Start from scratch**.

   Panel Manager opens **New automation**. Nothing is saved yet.

4. Under **When**, choose **Starts when**.

   For example, choose **When a door or window opens**, **When the panel is armed** or **At a time of day**.

5. Choose the details that the trigger asks for, for example **Which sensor?** or **Run at** and **On these days**.
6. To limit the hours, set **From** and **To** under **Only between**.
7. Under **Then**, choose the **Device** and the **Action**.

   For an action that turns something on, you can also set **Turn it off after**.

8. To add a second action, click **Add a second action**, and choose another device.
9. Click **Save**.

   Some automations ask **Save this automation?** and tell what the automation will do. Click **Save** to confirm.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
The list shows the new automation as a sentence. After it runs, the list shows **Ran at** and the time.
</Aside>

## Arm or disarm with an automation

The system master can choose **The alarm** under **Then**, with **Arm the panel** or **Disarm**. The installer cannot choose these actions, because the installer does not arm or disarm.

**Disarm** shows only if the installer allows it in **System options** with **Allow less secure automations**. If the installer turns this option off, automations that disarm, unlock or open do not run.

## Change, try, turn off or delete

Click an automation in the list to open it.

- To change it, change the **When** or **Then** fields. The bar at the bottom lists the changes. Click **Save**, or click **Undo**.
- To turn it off or on, use **Use this automation**. The change counts at once. An automation that is off stays in the list and does not run.
- To run its device actions once now, click **Try it now**. Save your changes first. **Try it now** does not arm or disarm. The result shows under **Last run**.
- To delete it, click **Delete this automation**, then **Delete**. You cannot undo this.

The panel keeps up to 16 automations. To add one more, delete one first.

## What the list shows

| The list shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ran at** and a time | The automation ran. |
| **Tried at** and a time | Someone clicked **Try it now**. |
| **Didn't work** | The automation ran, but a device did not answer. Open it to see the reason. The panel does not try again by itself. |
| **Waits until the clock is set** | A time automation waits until the panel knows the time. |
| **Can't run** | Something is missing, for example a device. Open the automation to see what to fix. |
| **Off: it won't run** | **Use this automation** is off. |

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Automations** is not in the menu. | Sign in as the system master or the installer. |
| The fields cannot be changed. | Disarm every partition, and wait until no alarm sounds. |
| **Someone changed automations meanwhile. The page now shows them as they are.** | Another person saved first. Check the list, and make your change again. |
| **Can't run: open it and pick the device again** | The device is gone or renamed. Open the automation, choose the device again, and click **Save**. |
| **Didn't work** | Check the device and its connection. Then click **Try it now**. |

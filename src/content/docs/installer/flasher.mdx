The Flasher is the desktop program that installs and updates the panel firmware over a USB cable. Use it before the first setup, and to update a panel that you cannot update from its own screen or from Panel Manager.

The Flasher guide has the full steps. Choose the task that fits:

<CardGrid>
  <LinkCard title="Install RoboAlarms" description="Put the firmware on a new panel. Install erases everything on the chip." href="/flasher/install/" />
  <LinkCard title="Update RoboAlarms" description="Update a panel and keep its settings." href="/flasher/update/" />
</CardGrid>

Before you connect a panel, have the matching firmware, a USB data cable and stable power. Disarm the panel and leave it idle on its own screen. See [Get started with the Flasher](/flasher/).

The Flasher also records optional parts, such as a 433 MHz receiver, a battery-backed clock or a Zigbee module. See [Connected parts](/flasher/connected-parts/).

After the install, the panel starts the first setup. See [Run the first setup](/installer/first-setup/).

Use **Smart Home** to control lights, switches, locks, thermostats and other household devices from the panel. You can also arrange the tiles, make rooms and protect a device with a master code.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- Home Assistant must share at least one device with the panel. See [Share Home Assistant entities](/integrations/home-assistant-sharing/).
- The rail shows **Smart Home** only while the panel has a shared device.
- To unlock a lock or open a door, cover or valve, you need a code that can disarm.
</Aside>

## What Smart Home shows

The panel can show these Home Assistant devices: lights, switches, locks, thermostats, covers, garage doors, fans, water valves, scenes and scripts. Home Assistant decides which devices the panel can see and control.

The row at the top has the tabs. **Favorites** is first, and the rooms follow. Devices without a room are in **Other**. If the panel has no rooms, that tab is **All devices**. The pencil at the end of the row starts Arrange.

A keypad panel shows the devices of its alarm panel.

## Control a device

<Steps>

1. Push **Smart Home** on the rail.
2. Push **Favorites** or a room tab.
3. Push the device tile.

   A light, switch or fan turns on or off. A scene or script runs. A lock locks or unlocks. A cover, garage door or valve opens or closes.

</Steps>

Lights, switches, fans, thermostats and scenes show the new state at once. Locks, garage doors, covers and valves show **Locking…**, **Unlocking…**, **Opening…** or **Closing…** until the device reports its new state.

Unlocking a lock asks for a code. Opening a garage door, cover or valve also asks for a code. An arm-only code or a guest code cannot unlock or open. A wrong code counts toward the keypad lockout.

If a cover or garage door is moving, a push stops it. If the device cannot stop, it turns back. A turn back to open asks for a code.

### Use the device sheet

Hold a tile, or push the arrow on the tile, to open the device sheet. The sheet shows the full name, the state and all controls of the device. For example, a light can have a brightness slider. A thermostat has its set point and mode. A cover can have **Open**, **Stop** and **Close**, and a fan can have speeds.

A wide tile can also show a control, such as a brightness slider or a temperature stepper. Home Assistant reports which controls each device has. Two devices of the same kind can show different controls.

<Aside type="danger" title="Warning">
Do not share a scene or script that you do not trust completely. A scene or script can unlock a lock or open a door without a code on the panel.
</Aside>

The panel asks for a code only for its own unlock and open commands. It cannot see what a Home Assistant scene or script does to other devices. Check each scene and script in Home Assistant before you share it.

## Use Favorites

Favorites is the first tab. It shows the devices that you use most.

<Steps>

1. Open **Smart Home**.
2. Push **Favorites**.
3. Push the pencil.
4. To add a device, push **Add**, and then push the device.
5. To take a device off Favorites, push its tile, and then push **Remove**.
6. Push **Done**.

</Steps>

**Remove** takes a tile off Favorites only. The device stays in its room.

## Arrange tiles

Arrange changes the layout of the tiles on this panel. It does not send a command to a device.

<Steps>

1. Open **Smart Home**, and then push the tab that you want to arrange.
2. Push the pencil.
3. Hold a tile and drag it to a new place.
4. To use the arrows instead, push the tile, and then push the left or right arrow.
5. To change the size, push the tile, and then push **Small** or **Wide**.
6. Push **Done**.

</Steps>

A wide tile has space for a control, such as a brightness slider.

## Make and order rooms

The rooms belong to the panel. At first, the panel uses the areas from Home Assistant as its rooms. A device follows its Home Assistant area until you put it in a room on the panel. The panel can have up to 24 rooms.

### Put a device in a room

<Steps>

1. Open **Smart Home**, and then push the pencil.
2. Push the device tile.
3. Push **Room**.

   The panel shows **Room for** and the device name.

4. Push a room.

   To make a new room for the device, push **New room** and type a name. To take the device out of all rooms, push **No room**.

5. Push **Done**.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/smart-home-room-picker.png" alt="Room for Porch Light, with Room from its source, four rooms and a check mark beside Patio" caption="The check mark shows the room of the device; Room from its source makes it follow Home Assistant again." size="small" />

**Room from its source** puts the device back in its Home Assistant area. The device then moves when Home Assistant moves it.

You can also add devices from a room tab. In Arrange, push **Add** in the room tab, and then push a device. The device moves to that room.

### Add, rename, order or remove rooms

<Steps>

1. Open **Smart Home**, and then push the pencil.
2. Push **Rooms**.

   The panel shows the rooms in the order of their tabs.

3. To add a room, push **New room** and type a name.
4. To change a room, push the room.
5. Push **Rename**, **Earlier**, **Later** or **Remove room**.
6. Push **Done**.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/smart-home-room-actions.png" alt="The Patio room sheet with Rename, Earlier, Later and Remove room" caption="Earlier and Later move the room tab one place to the left or right." size="small" />

**Remove room** removes the room only. Its devices stay on the panel and show in **Other**. Favorites does not change. Two rooms cannot have the same name.

## Protect a device with a master code

You can make a device ask for a master code before each change. You can also make Arrange ask for a master code. These settings are on the alarm panel only. From the factory, they are all off.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- You need a system master, partition master or installer code.
- **Settings > Smart Home** shows only while the panel has a shared device.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings > Smart Home**.
2. Enter a master or installer code, if the panel asks for one.
3. Under **Devices that ask for a master code before a change**, turn on the switch of the device.
4. To protect the layout, turn on **Arranging asks for a master code**.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/smart-home-settings.png" alt="Settings Smart Home page with the Arranging asks for a master code switch and one switch for each device" caption="The devices are in groups by room, and each switch protects one device." size="medium" />

A protected device shows a key on its tile. When someone changes it, the panel asks for a master code. After a correct master code, more changes on the same panel go through for 90 seconds. A lock still asks for a code each time it unlocks. A garage door, cover or valve still asks for a code each time it opens.

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/smart-home-master-code.png" alt="Code pad titled Change Hallway with the line Hallway asks for a master code" caption="The line under the title tells you that this device asks for a master code." size="small" />

**Arranging asks for a master code** covers tile places, tile sizes, Favorites and rooms. When it is on, the pencil opens the **Arrange Smart Home** code pad first.

## What the tile messages mean

| Message | What it means | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Locking…**, **Unlocking…**, **Opening…**, **Closing…**, **Stopping…** | The panel sent the command. The device has not reported its new state yet. | Wait for the device to report. |
| **Didn't lock**, **Didn't open** and other **Didn't** messages | The device refused the command, or the command failed. | Look at the device. Push the tile again if it is safe. |
| **Not confirmed** | The device did not answer, so the panel cannot tell if it acted. | Look at the device itself before you try again. This is important for a lock or a door. |
| **Offline** or **Offline since** a time | The panel cannot reach the device. Its controls do not work. | Make sure the device is on and online in Home Assistant. |
| **Jammed** | The lock reports that it is jammed. | Look at the lock. |
| **Ran at** a time | The time the scene last ran. | No action. |

The panel never repeats a command by itself.

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Smart Home** is not on the rail. | Make sure Home Assistant is paired and shares at least one device. See [Share Home Assistant entities](/integrations/home-assistant-sharing/). |
| A device is missing. | Make sure Home Assistant shares the device with the panel. |
| A device is in the wrong room. | Put it in a room on the panel. See [Put a device in a room](#put-a-device-in-a-room). |
| A control, such as brightness or fan speed, is missing. | The device does not report that control to Home Assistant. |
| The panel shows **Only a master code can change it**. | The device is protected. Enter a system master, partition master or installer code. |
| The panel shows **It is still busy with the last change**. | Wait until the device finishes the last change. |
| The panel shows **It can't be reached right now**. | Make sure the device is online in Home Assistant. |

You cannot rename or hide a device on the panel. Change the name in Home Assistant.

To make Smart Home the screen that the panel rests on, see [Screen and sound](/user/display-sound/#choose-the-start-screen).

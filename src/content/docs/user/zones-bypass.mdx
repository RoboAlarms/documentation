A **zone** is one sensor with a name, for example a door, a window or a motion sensor. Use this page to find a zone that is open, and to bypass a zone before you arm.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- To bypass, you need a code that can bypass. **Arm only** and **Guest** codes cannot bypass.
- The panel must be disarmed to bypass a zone.
</Aside>

## Find what is open

<Steps>

1. Push **Zones** on the rail.

   The panel shows a card for each zone with its name and state. The line below the title counts the zones that are open and bypassed.

2. Push **Open** to show only the zones that are open.

3. Close the door or window that the card names. For a motion sensor, wait until the card shows **No motion**.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/zones-list.png" alt="The Zones screen with the All, Open, Bypassed and Trouble filters and a card for each zone" caption="Each card shows the zone name, its state and a Bypass or Unbypass button." />

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
When you push **Open**, the panel shows no zones. On the **Security** screen, the state text shows **Ready to arm**.
</Aside>

### Zone states

| State on the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Closed**, **No motion**, **Normal** or **Dry** | The zone is ready. |
| **Open**, **Motion** or another active state | The sensor detects something now. |
| **Bypassed** | The zone is left out of the next arming. |
| **Low battery**, **Tamper**, **Not reporting** or **Sensor fault** | The zone has a trouble. Push **Trouble** to show all zones with a trouble. |
| **Unknown** | On a keypad panel: the keypad cannot reach the alarm panel. |

A zone with a trouble is not the same as an open door. With **Not reporting**, the panel cannot get the state of the sensor. See [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting/).

## Bypass one zone

A bypassed zone is left out of the protection the next time you arm. All bypasses clear when you disarm.

<Aside type="danger" title="Warning">
Bypass a zone only when you know why it is open. A bypassed zone does not cause a burglary alarm while the panel is armed.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Push **Zones** on the rail.

2. On the card of the zone, push **Bypass**.

   The panel shows a code pad with the zone name, for example **Bypass Front Door**.

   <PanelShot src="/images/panel/zones-bypass-pad.png" alt="The Bypass Front Door code pad over the Zones screen" caption="The title of the code pad names the zone that you bypass." size="small" />

3. Enter your code.

4. Push the check mark.

   The card shows **Bypassed**, and the button changes to **Unbypass**.

5. Arm the panel. See [Arm and disarm](/user/arming-disarming/).

</Steps>

To put the zone back before you arm, push **Unbypass**, enter your code and push the check mark.

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the result">
Push **Bypassed**. The panel shows the zones that are bypassed.
</Aside>

### Zones that you cannot bypass

A zone without a **Bypass** button cannot be bypassed. Fire and CO zones cannot be bypassed. Other 24-hour zones can be bypassed only if the installer allows it. The **Bypass** button is also not shown while the panel is armed.

## Bypass open zones when you arm

If a zone is open when you arm, the code pad shows **Bypass open**. Push **Bypass open**, then push the check mark. The panel bypasses all open zones that can be bypassed, and arms. Before you use it, push **Open** on the **Zones** screen to see which zones it leaves out.

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The card has no **Bypass** button. | The zone cannot be bypassed, or the panel is armed. Close the zone, or disarm first. |
| The panel does not accept your code for a bypass. | Your code cannot bypass. Ask the person who manages users. |
| A zone has the wrong name or reacts to the wrong sensor. | Tell the installer. The installer changes zones in [Zones](/installer/zones/). |
| A zone often shows **Not reporting**. | Tell the installer. The sensor or its connection needs attention. |

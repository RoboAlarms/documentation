Use **Settings > Screen and sound** to set how this panel sounds and looks. These settings apply to this panel only. A keypad panel has its own settings.

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
- You do not need a code. Anyone can change these settings.
- All partitions must be disarmed, with no alarm. Settings open only then.
</Aside>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/display-sound-settings.png" alt="Screen and sound settings with Chime volume, Voice volume, Beeps volume and Key clicks" caption="The Sound section is at the top of the page, and the Screen section follows below." />

## Sound

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Chime volume** | Sets the volume of the door chime. |
| **Voice volume** | Sets the volume of the spoken announcements. It does not change alarm sounds. |
| **Beeps volume** | Sets the volume of the entry and exit beeps, trouble beeps and key clicks. |
| **Key clicks** | Turns the short click for each touch on or off. |
| **Announcements** | Opens the voice settings. See [Voice announcements](#voice-announcements). |

Alarm, fire and CO sounds always play at full volume. No setting on this page makes them quieter.

To turn the chime on or off, push the bell beside the gear at the top of the screen. See [Turn the chime on or off](/user/chime/).

## Voice announcements

The panel can say what happens in words. For example, it says when it arms, disarms or starts an entry time. The voice needs a voice pack on the panel. The voice speaks English (United States), whatever the screen language is.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings > Screen and sound**.
2. Push **Announcements**.
3. Turn on **Announcements**.
4. Turn on **Status messages** to hear the arming, armed, disarmed, entry and not ready messages.
5. Turn on **Zone chime** to hear the spoken name of a zone after its chime.
6. Push **Preview** to hear a sample.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/display-sound-announcements.png" alt="Announcements page with the Announcements, Status messages and Zone chime switches and the Preview button" caption="Status messages and Zone chime work only while the Announcements switch is on." size="medium" />

**Preview** works while Announcements is off, and it does not turn Announcements on. The installer sets the spoken name of each zone. A walk test always speaks the zone names when the voice is ready.

If the panel has no voice pack, **Announcements** shows **Needs a voice pack** and cannot be turned on. If the voice does not work, the panel shows **Voice is unavailable.**

## Choose the start screen

The start screen is the screen that the panel rests on. You can choose **Security** or **Smart Home**. This setting shows only while **Smart Home** is on the rail.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings > Screen and sound**.
2. Under **Screen**, push **Start screen**.
3. Push **Security** or **Smart Home**.

</Steps>

<PanelShot src="/images/panel/display-sound-start-screen.png" alt="Start screen choices: Security, The alarm state and arming, and Smart Home, Your devices; Security while armed" caption="The check mark shows the start screen that is in use." size="small" />

While the panel is disarmed, it goes back to the start screen at these times:

- After it starts.
- After nobody touches it for 90 seconds.
- Under the screen saver.

While the panel is armed, it rests on **Security**. An alarm or an entry time always shows **Security**.

## Screen

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Selects **Automatic**, **Dark** or **Light**. **Automatic** is dark during the night hours and light by day. If **Night hours** is off, the screen stays light. |
| **High contrast** | Makes the colors stronger and puts an outline on each button. |
| **Brightness** | Sets the normal brightness of the screen. |
| **Screen saver** | Sets when the dimmed photo with the time and the alarm state starts. **Screen saver brightness** on the same page makes it dimmer. At 0, the screen goes dark until a touch wakes it. |
| **Screen off** | Sets when the screen goes dark until a touch wakes it. |
| **Night hours** | Sets a daily time range when the screen is dimmer and goes dark sooner. |

Night hours need a set clock. Night hours change the screen only. They do not arm or disarm the alarm.

A touch wakes the screen. A screen saver delay does not change an entry or exit time.

## Clock format

To choose a 12-hour or 24-hour clock, open **Settings > Time and language > Clock**. Anyone can change the clock format. See [Wi-Fi, network and time](/user/network-time/#set-the-time-zone-language-and-time-server).

## If it does not work

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings** does not open. | Disarm all partitions and silence any alarm. |
| **Announcements** is not on the page. | This panel has no voice. Ask the installer. |
| **Announcements** cannot be turned on. | The voice pack is missing or does not work. Ask the installer to update the panel. |
| **Start screen** is not on the page. | The panel has no shared devices, so **Smart Home** is not on the rail. |
| Night hours do not start. | Set the clock in **Settings > Time and language**. |
